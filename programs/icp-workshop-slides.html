Houston BloomEvent outline

Where it started

One hand went up.

At a networking event in September, a speaker asked the room whether anyone knew someone in his ICP's sphere of influence. One person raised a hand. That is how you get in, he said.

This workshop makes that question searchable, so the right person does not have to be the one who happens to raise a hand.
