Community Legibility Spec

How event capture works

From the individual's calendar to the community data commons and back again.

Four phases

01

Curate

The individual adds events to their own calendar.

02

Capture

Each event is converted into a record on their own system.

03

Contribute

They see each event and say yes, no or edit before anything goes to the commons.

04

Broadcast and return

The commons broadcasts what members chose to share. Each individual approves what comes back.

An example

01

Their own protocol

They add events to their calendar, or bookmark them in an app with an RSS feed.

02

An AI agent routine

Their agent gathers those events, curates them and delivers them to the community.

03

Shared with the commons

The events go to the community data commons over a private pathway.

Signal encryption
04

A community broadcast

Every community broadcasts all its members' events. Anyone taps in with an RSS feed.

Two ways data comes in

On your own

Their own systems

The individual adds events to their own systems, and captures them on their own computer.

At a community event

A facilitated room

What people share goes onto one sheet, and each person chooses who sees each line.

You consent to what leaves.

Every step where data moves has a consent gate.

It all reads offline.

What comes back is a folder the individual keeps: a spreadsheet, a ZIM file for Kiwix, and an HTML page.

Download a ZIM readerKiwix, for desktop and phone.get.kiwix.org

Many people, one commons.

Each member contributes their weekly mappings and curations of events. The commons broadcasts what they chose to share.

Many commons, one mesh.

Each community's commons projects its own events. Anyone can tap into the others' broadcasts.
