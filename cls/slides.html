Why

Make your thinking legible to others.

Keep a tight pulse on what your system is doing, sending or needing.

Cancel your subscriptions.

Before you start

Install three things

Each has an Install button on the Getting started page.

  1. Node.js 18 or later
  2. Claude Code, or Codex in the ChatGPT desktop app
  3. Kiwix, for offline files

ThenMake a new, empty folder and open your agent in it.

CheckStep 1 checks your Node.js version.

The setup prompt

One prompt does steps 1 to 9.

The packetYour name and email
+ prompt → Your contacts, card, dashboard and manifest 
Set me up with the Community Legibility Spec packet at <packet>.
My name is <Your Name> and my email is <you@example.org>.
Follow <packet>/SETUP.md steps 1 to 9 in this folder, in order, using the By hand commands.
After each step, check the output against "You should see" and stop if it differs.
Do not send anything anywhere. When you are done, show me my dashboard and tell me where my card is.
