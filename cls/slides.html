Community Legibility Spec

At the end you have your own folder with your contacts in it, your contact card, a dashboard, and a manifest that starts with this packet and logs what you share.

Why

Why

  • Make your thinking legible to others.
  • Keep a tight pulse on what your system is doing, sending or needing.
  • Cancel your subscriptions.

What you will make

What you end up with

The packet is a folder you copy. One prompt to your agent, or eleven steps by hand, turn it into your contacts, your card and your manifest, all on your own machine.

Before you start

What each one is for
Node.js
Runs the packet’s tools. Version 18 or later; step 1 checks it.
Claude Code
An AI agent that works with the files on your computer. It runs the setup prompt. Codex in the ChatGPT desktop app also works.
Kiwix
Opens a shareable sent as one packed .zim file, offline, on a phone, tablet or computer. For someone who keeps a library or has no steady connection.
Tailscale
A private network between your computer and a community server, for Local Assembly. Local Assembly is not built yet; its setup prompts are on its page.

Step 1 of 11

Check Node.js

Check that Node.js 18 or later is installed.

See the prompt
Check that Node.js 18 or later is installed.
You should see
`v18` or higher, such as `v24.13.0`. If not, install the LTS version from nodejs.org and open a new terminal.

Step 2 of 11

Make your folder

Make 00_inbox, 00_contacts and notes in this folder.

See the prompt
Make 00_inbox, 00_contacts and notes in this folder.
You should see
three empty folders. `00_inbox` is where you drop anything you want captured: a contact card, a note about someone, an export. `00_contacts` is where your people are kept. `notes` is for things you make. They are yours: an update to the packet never touches them.

Step 3 of 11

Bring in the packet

Copy <packet>/PACKET.md here.

See the prompt
Copy <packet>/PACKET.md here.
You should see
`PACKET.md` in your folder. The tools stay in the packet, in `<packet>/contacts-tools`, and are updated there.

Step 4 of 11

See the prompt
Copy <packet>/skills into .claude/skills here, then write .claude/cls.json naming my folders:

Step 9 of 11

Open your dashboard

Check my contacts folder, build my dashboard and my activity view, then open them.

See the prompt

You should see
`PASS · 7 schemas describe every field and pass their own examples · 2 people ...`, then `2 people · 0 organizations · 0 exchanges ...`, then `wrote activity.html · ...`, and the dashboard with you and Sam on it. `00_contacts/activity.html` is your activity: everything that passed between you and people, and everything you mapped, made, shared and got back, newest first.
