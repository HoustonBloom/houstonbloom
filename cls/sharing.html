Sharing something

Something you make to send gets a row in your manifest. Each step after that is logged: who it went to, whether it was published, what came back.

Sam Lee and the files are made up. The ids, printed lines and log lines came from a real run of the tool on a test folder. Nothing is sent by the tool. You send it.

The picture

One row per step: the command, what it printed, and what changed in the manifest. Ids are shown by their first 8 characters; the tool accepts that too.

The send is the step that widens who may see it.Sent to several people: --to "Sam Lee, Robin Park", one row, each name added. Sending a file never made logs the make first.

What each field holds

kind
shareable, for something made to send and for something that came back.
visibility
Starts ["private"] on make. Each send drops private and adds each person, and public with --public. Something received starts visible to the people it came from.
madeAt
The day it was made or received.
sent
Each send: on, the day; to, the names; public, when it was published.
from
Who sent back something you received.
relates_to
The id of the shareable this one answers, so the two read as one exchange.
project
The project it belongs to, when you give one.
hash
A fingerprint of the contents, to tell an edit from a touch. Never shared.
offered
The day session end offered a transformation note. It is not offered again.
notes
Transformation notes attached with note: the day and the note's path.

The transformation note is a short file you fill in, from the template skills/share/transformation.md. Its parts: what I sent or made, what came back, what changed in my work, what changed in theirs, and impact. "Nothing" is an answer.
