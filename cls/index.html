Community Legibility Spec

Your people and your work, kept as plain files on your own computer, with a record of what you share and with whom.

  • Make your thinking legible to others.
  • Keep a tight pulse on what your system is doing, sending or needing.
  • Cancel your subscriptions.

The packet you install is not published yet. When it is, the link goes here.

Where it stands

Five conditions. The spec is done when all five are met.

1. Your last contact book
close The contacts format is drafted and its checks pass. Shown here as a draft: a person and their connections.
2. Make your system legible
started The tools that map your folders and log what you share are built and tested. Four folders are mapped.
3. See your progress over time
can be met now A changelog, tagged by project, draws the activity view on this site.
4. Share your thinking
started The share tool is built and tested on a test folder. It has not yet gone to a person.
5. Co-create with others
not met

What you end up with

The packet is a folder you copy. One prompt to your agent, or eleven steps by hand, turn it into your contacts, your card and your manifest, all on your own machine.

The person setting up below is Priya Shah, and their first contact is Sam Lee. Both are made up to show the shape. You need Node.js 18 or later. Nothing in the packet sends anything anywhere.

What each field holds

contacts
In cls.json. The full path of your contacts folder.
manifest
In cls.json. The full path of your manifest file. It must sit outside every folder it maps.
me
In 00_contacts/settings.json. Your own id, set by step 5. Lets an intake note say me for you.
N, FN
On your card. Your name split into family and given, then your name as you write it.
EMAIL
On your card. One line for each address on your record.
TEL, URL, ORG, TITLE
On your card when your record has them: phone, links, where you work, your job title.
CATEGORIES, NOTE
On your card when your record has them: your tags and your description.
UID
On your card. urn:00contacts: plus your id, so the card comes back to the same record.

What the file looks like inside

JSON, a plain text format. This is .claude/cls.json, written by step 4.

{
  "contacts": "/home/priya/cls/00_contacts",
  "manifest": "/home/priya/cls/manifest/manifest.json",
  "spec": "/home/priya/cls/.claude/skills/map/Manifest Spec.md",
  "skills": "/home/priya/cls/.claude/skills",
  "inbox": "/home/priya/cls/00_inbox"
}

And the card, per_priya-shah.vcf, written by step 6. A plain text vCard 3.0 that any phone opens.

BEGIN:VCARD
VERSION:3.0
N:Shah;Priya;;;
FN:Priya Shah
EMAIL;TYPE=INTERNET:priya@example.org
UID:urn:00contacts:per_priya-shah
END:VCARD

Start

  1. Getting startedEvery prompt to set up the packet, in order, and what to install first: Node.js, an AI agent, Kiwix and Tailscale.

Your contacts

  1. What a contact isOne folder per person or organization. Inside, one text file that says who they are, and one line for each exchange you had with them.
  2. How sure we areEvery person and organization gets one of three levels: verified, confident or unsure. The sure ones go into your records on their own; the unsure ones wait for you to look.
  3. How someone likes to be reachedA few lines on a person's file: how they like to be reached and talked to, and how things made for them should look.
  4. ProjectsOne list of every project, each with an id that never changes. People and organizations point at the id, so a project's name lives in one place.
  5. Wants and offersA want is something still open between you and someone: they need something from you. An offer is something they put out. Both use the Murmurations offers and wants shape.

Your manifest

  1. What the manifest isOne file on your computer. One row per top-level folder you name, and one per thing you make to share.
  2. Sharing somethingSomething you make to send gets a row in your manifest. Each step after that is logged: who it went to, whether it was published, what came back.
  3. What a person getsA redacted copy of your manifest for one person: when you were active together, and the rows that name them. It is the only file that leaves.
  4. Impact reportA report on what someone's information did to your work after it reached you. Drafted from your records, finished by you, sent by you.
