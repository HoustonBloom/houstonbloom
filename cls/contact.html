What a contact is

One folder per person or organization. Inside, one text file that says who they are, and one line for each exchange you had with them.

The people below are made up to show the shape. Priya Shah owns the folder; everyone else is a contact. Contract 0.2.0 for a person, 0.1.0 for an organization and an exchange.

What each field holds

id
per_ plus the name they use, or org_ plus the organization's name. Given once and never changed; everything else joins on it.
linked_schemas
The formats this file follows: the Murmurations profile first, then this folder's add-on.
name
The name they use, from their own signature or profile. Never guessed from a calendar title or an email address.
primary_url
Their own site. Left out when they have none.
urls
Any further links, as the card gives them, so a card with two links comes back out with two.
email
Every address they have written from.
telephone
Phone numbers, as the card gives them.
title
A person's job title, as their card or signature gives it.
organization
Where a person works. An org_ id when the organization is on record, otherwise its name as the card gives it.
description
One or two sentences on who they are, in your words.
tags
Words you use to find them.
relationships
Who or what they are connected to: a relation and a person, an organization or a project, the Murmurations triple.
visibility
Who may see this file. local means it never leaves your computer.
met_through
How you met a person, in a phrase.
first_exchange, last_exchange
Worked out from their exchanges, never typed.
elsewhere
Where the same person also lives: a profile, a contact card. A pointer, never a copy.
filed_at, filed_by
When this file was written, and what wrote it: you, a routine, the processor.
confidence
How sure you can be this is one real person or organization: verified, confident or unsure, and why. More.
contact_preferences
A person's only. How they like to be reached and talked to, and how things made for them should look. More.

An exchange

What passed between you: an email, a call, a file, a set of links. One line each in records/<id>/exchanges.jsonl, added to and never rewritten.

Every line says when it happened (at) and, apart from that, when and how it was filed. The same event filed twice gets the same id.
