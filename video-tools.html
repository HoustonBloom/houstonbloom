    Local Legibility Packet

    Video Editing Tools

    Drop a recording in a folder and ask your AI. It writes one page of who spoke and what is worth cutting, then stills, clips and posts.

    Bloom Studio is the pilot, on Windows: these tools, a one-step install and a start page. Ask in the Discord to join.

    Join the pilot↗ Ask in the Discord How it works↓
    • Runs on your computer
    • Nothing uploaded or posted
    • You approve every cut

    How it works

    From recording to posts.

    Each step writes a file the next one reads. Where judgement is needed, it stops.

    1. Stage 1 · You

      You drop it in

      Your recording and its captions, into your inbox.

      • you, at the folder

      Then type /process

    2. Stage 2

      Your AI runs it

      Captions, a sound check, a full read, and a still per section.

      • transcribe
      • noise-check
      • readthrough
      • stills

      Makes readthrough.html

    3. Stage 3 · You

      It ends on one page

      Who was up, when, and the lines worth cutting. You fix names and pick.

      • you, at the page

      Makes a prompt for your AI

    4. Stage 4

      Then you ask for more

      Stills, a Reel, a tightened take, captions. Each shows a plan first.

      • shorts
      • tighten
      • captions

      Makes clips and frames

    The posting plan runs only if you say yes: a date, one ask and a caption per post. Nothing is posted.

    What to ask for

    Five things to ask for.

    Type one to your AI in the Bloom Studio folder.

    • /process

      Reads what is new in your inbox and ends on the readthrough page.

    • /plan

      A posting plan for one recording: which clip goes out on which day, with one ask and a caption for each.

    • /make

      Stills, a clip, a tightened take or captions, from any recording. It shows you a plan and one frame before it renders.

    • /updates

      Checks for newer tools and gets them when you say yes. It waits for any job still running.

    • /help

      When something goes wrong: a short report to send to Megan. She replies with a prompt you paste back.

    • End card and QRComing soon

      A closing card with a scannable link, added to a finished clip.

    Step by step

    The four steps

    1. 1
      You

      Get Bloom Studio

      Ask in the Discord to join the pilot. Once you are added, clone HoustonBloom/bloom-studio with GitHub Desktop. A clone updates itself with /updates. A zip download cannot.

    2. 2
      You

      Get your AI assistant

      Install Claude and sign in. Use Claude Code or Cowork, so it can work with files on your computer, and give it the Bloom Studio folder. Download Claude.

      Show the prompt
      prompt-to-meet-your-ai.txt 
      I am setting up Bloom Studio, a set of video tools in this folder. Read AGENTS.md here, then tell me in two lines what you can do with it.
    3. 3
      You paste, your AI works

      Set it up with one prompt

      Your AI installs the tools, makes your Video Work folder with its inbox, and points the start page at it. It took 1 min 42 s and 2 min 10 s on two test runs, and checks for 950 MB free first.

      Show the prompt
      prompt-to-set-up.txt 
      Set up Bloom Studio for me. In this Bloom Studio folder: run install.bat -NoPause and wait until every tool says ready. Then make a folder called Video Work in my Documents folder, with a folder called inbox inside it. Then run point.bat -Workspace "<the full path of Video Work>" -NoShortcut -NoOpen. When it is done, tell me to reopen this page.
    4. 4
      You paste, your AI works

      Your first recording

      Put a recording in Video Work/inbox, with its captions if you have them, then paste this. With captions, plan for about 30 minutes on a 95-minute recording. Without, add about 30 more.

      Show the prompt
      prompt-for-a-recording.txt 
      /process the new recording in my inbox

    If something fails, type /help.

    It writes a short report to send to Megan, and she replies with a prompt you paste back. Your AI is told to stop and name the step, not guess.

    The start page

    A start page that holds no data.

    START-HERE.html opens from disk and reads your Video Work folder each time. Close it, and nothing is stored in it.

    • Setup

      One slide per step, each with its prompt and a Copy button. A step ticks only when the page can see it is done.

      where you begin

    • Gallery

      One poster per recording, linking to its readthrough and its posting plan.

      fills as you process

    • Explore

      A map of your recordings and the pages made from them.

      orbit or graph

    • Inbox banner

      What the last check found in your inbox, with the /process prompt ready to copy.

      written by /process

    It is a relational space. That is a template from the Community Legibility Spec: it holds nobody's data. You point it at your own, change it, and share it back. Nothing leaves your computer.

    How they behave

    Four rules the tools keep.

    • The plan is the deliverable

      Every cutting and framing decision lives in a JSON file naming its source, its segments and its reasons. A render is disposable because its plan is not. A clip whose plan is lost is a clip nobody can explain.

    • Measured, not eyeballed

      Geometry off pixels. Loudness off the file. Cut points off the waveform at 20ms, never off transcript timings, which are wrong by tens of milliseconds and will slice the middle of a word.

    • It proposes, you choose

      Nothing here decides what is worth cutting. The tools narrow three hours to a page of candidates, and every one of them stays a candidate.

    • Nothing is published

      These tools write files. No upload, no post, no push. What leaves an account is your decision, per action.

    What it will not do

    • Fetch from a platform. A recording arrives as a file from whoever owns it.
    • Recognise faces. Nothing here knows who is in a frame. You look before anything goes out.
    • Promise clean captions. They are machine transcribed. Proper nouns are the least reliable part.
    • Measure what lands. Length and framing are craft defaults with reasons, not measured optimums.

    Four rules the tools keep · 2 of 4

    Measured, not eyeballed

    Geometry off pixels. Loudness off the file. Cut points off the waveform at 20ms, never off transcript timings, which are wrong by tens of milliseconds and will slice the middle of a word.

    Four rules the tools keep · 3 of 4

    It proposes, you choose

    Nothing here decides what is worth cutting. The tools narrow three hours to a page of candidates, and every one of them stays a candidate.

    Four rules the tools keep · 4 of 4

    Nothing is published

    These tools write files. No upload, no post, no push. What leaves an account is your decision, per action.
