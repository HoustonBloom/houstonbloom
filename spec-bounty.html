Program

Super Connector bounty

A public prize for whoever trains and brings in the most people who show up. Claiming it is how the activity ledger gets tested.

Overview

Runs on
Dual-Ledger Authentication
Status
Pending, not funded

How it works

Shares are logged
When someone shares over the network at an event, the community keeps that log
Entrants submit their own ledger
For the events they attended
The two records must match
The same share at the same time in both logs; that match is the dual ledger
Commons contributions count
Data earns points when someone else pulls it from the commons

Settled

Turnout is the risk
No people, no events, no signal
Depth over reach
Repeat attendance is the stronger signal
The leaderboard is the marketing
The finalists are the content

Still open

Funding
There is no money for the prize yet
The reward
