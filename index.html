    Some perspective

    Indigenous Peoples

    98% of human history

    ~17,000 mothers ago

    ~300,000 yrs

    Trust, reciprocity, shared knowledge at Dunbar scale. Tribal groups governed by proximity, kinship, and the relationships you could hold in your head. No one coordinated from above because no one needed to.

    Subject

    1.7% of human history

    ~280 mothers ago

    ~5,400 yrs

    Writing. Math. The Printing Press. Kings, priests, states. Coordination above Dunbar scale required vertical structures.

    Consumer

    0.05% of human history

    ~7 mothers ago

    ~175 yrs

    Industrial revolution. Owning became production. People became consumers. The top changed hands. The structure never did.

    User

    0.008% of human history

    1 mother ago

    ~25 yrs

    The internet. Owning became renting. Production became data. Consumers became users. Five companies became the town square.

    Citizen

    Now

    Gen AI. Local-first. Open models. For the first time, the easier option can be the lateral one. What happens next is a design choice.

    Community Legibility Spec

    The technology Houston Bloom runs on

    A way for a community to see itself clearly enough to match people to each other, without anyone holding everyone’s data. Your work stays on your machine. Only its shape travels.

    What actually moves is a redacted manifest: an opaque identifier, a timestamp, and the design principle the work stands on. No names, no paths, no content. The work itself never moves, so you cannot leak it by contributing.

    Authentication without an authority. A community broadcasts an anonymised record of its activity. Each participant keeps a private, append-only ledger. Proving membership is just the comparison of the two: you show your record overlaps the broadcast, without the community ever naming you. Either side can end it by deleting its own half, so leaving is always available.

    Instead of giving you my ID attached to my email, what if I gave you the timestamps of what I shared at a networking event? I have my timestamps. The community has them too. It becomes proof of action, anonymised.
